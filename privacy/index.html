Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

This policy explains how PIXORA Play and the PIXORA website handle information. The app section describes PIXORA Play’s ad-free version with optional permanent colouring packs. It does not describe PIXORA Budget or PIXORA Track.

PIXORA Play: a summary for parents

PIXORA Play is a child-focused colouring, drawing and puzzle app. It has no advertising, subscription or Premium tier. Original content is free; extra colouring packs can be bought once. There is no child account, login, chat or social sharing feature.

Purchases are presented behind a parent arithmetic check and completed through Google Play. The check is a safeguard against accidental access, not identity verification or a substitute for Google Play purchase authentication.

Artwork and progress on your device

Colouring pictures you save, your drawing canvas and puzzle progress are stored in the app’s local storage. PIXORA Play does not upload these drawings or gameplay progress to PIXORA or RevenueCat. View Picture displays an in-app preview; it does not export to your photo library or request media access.

Clearing app data or uninstalling may remove local work. Android backup is enabled, so device backup or transfer may preserve or restore some app data, depending on your Android settings and device. We cannot promise that uninstalling erases every backup. Use your device’s backup controls to manage those copies.

Purchases and RevenueCat

We use Google Play Billing and RevenueCat to retrieve products and prices, validate purchases, check permanent pack entitlements and restore purchases. Google Play handles payment information. PIXORA Play does not receive your payment-card details.

RevenueCat creates an anonymous app-user identifier when no account identifier is supplied. It processes purchase history, product and entitlement information, purchase tokens and technical information such as app/SDK and operating-system versions, device information, locale and network connection information. Purchase records also support RevenueCat’s purchase reporting.

The purchase SDK starts when the Android app opens, before a parent enters the purchase screen, to check whether paid packs are already owned. This can involve network requests even if you do not make a new purchase. Free packs do not depend on a successful purchase-service connection. Existing access can use information cached by the SDK where available; restoration or refreshed access may need a connection.

RevenueCat processes app-user data on PIXORA’s behalf. Its services may process data outside your country, including in the United States, under its contractual data-protection arrangements. See RevenueCat’s privacy information, its Data Processing Addendum and Google’s privacy policy.

RevenueCat also uses Google Block Store to back up an anonymous purchase identifier when purchases exist, with cloud backup requested where supported. It can recover or associate that identifier during purchase/restoration flows to help retain access after reinstall or device changes. This is separate from backup of your drawings.

Information the app does not request

PIXORA Play does not ask for a name, email address, phone number, date of birth or account login. It does not request access to contacts, location, camera, microphone or your photos/media library. It has no advertising-ID permission and does not invoke advertising-ID or attribution collection APIs. The anonymous purchase identifier described above is separate from an advertising ID.

The parent challenge answer is checked in memory and is not saved or sent to a server. The app’s font is bundled locally, so starting Play does not request fonts from Google. If you choose to email us, we receive your email address and the information you include; please do not send a child’s personal details unnecessarily.

Retention, deletion and your rights

Local artwork and progress remain until removed through app/device controls, subject to backup and restore as described above. Purchase and entitlement records may remain with RevenueCat and Google Play to provide access and restoration and to meet applicable contractual, accounting, fraud-prevention or legal requirements. Uninstalling does not delete store transaction records.

For access, correction, deletion or other privacy enquiries, a parent or guardian can contact hello@pixora.es. We may need enough purchase information to locate the relevant record; do not send payment-card details. We can request deletion of records we control, subject to applicable retention requirements, but cannot promise deletion of Google Play records we do not control. Deletion may affect our ability to restore pack access.

Depending on applicable law, you may also request restriction or portability, object to processing, or withdraw consent where consent applies. You may contact the Spanish Data Protection Authority, AEPD, about a concern.

Security

RevenueCat uses TLS to protect data in transit. Payment details are handled by Google Play. Protect access to your device and its backups; no storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security.

Website and external pages

Opening Privacy Policy or Rate PIXORA Play opens an external page. The browser, website host and destination services receive connection information, such as an IP address and browser details. Their processing is separate from local gameplay.

The PIXORA website contact form sends information you choose to provide to Formspree so we can respond. Email enquiries are used to handle your request and retained as needed for the enquiry and relevant business records. Please see Formspree’s privacy policy.

Other website pages currently load Google Analytics, which can use cookies and process website usage and technical information. This Privacy Policy page does not load Google Analytics. Website pages, including this page, load Poppins from Google Fonts and images from PIXORA’s image host; those requests disclose connection information to the services involved. The Android app does not include this website analytics code. See Google’s privacy information.

Website enquiries are processed to respond to requests. Purchase processing provides requested functionality and supports applicable legal obligations. Where consent is required, it must be obtained separately; completing an arithmetic challenge does not itself establish legal consent.
